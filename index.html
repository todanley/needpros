How it works

  1. Send the form. Pick a category or describe the job, and include a deadline if you have one.
  2. We reply by email with a written scope and a price — hourly or fixed — or we decline. There is no fee for a declined request.
  3. If you accept, you pay the quoted amount. The $50 cash voucher comes off your first job. Then we do the work and send it back.

We are a small studio. If we use a contractor for part of a job, the quote and the delivery still come from us. Payment is by card, through a payment link we send after you accept the quote. If that link isn’t ready yet, we’ll say so in the reply and we won’t ask you to pay until it is.

FAQ

Is the work done by AI?

No. The work is done by real human professionals, not by AI. Every delivery is checked with an AI-detection tool before it reaches you. We don’t say that makes a file “100% AI-free” or “guaranteed not AI.” Hourly jobs are tracked with desktop time-tracking software, and you see the hours logged.

Who does the work?

A small studio. We are not a public board of named freelancers. If we need a contractor for part of a job, the quote and the delivery still come from us. In either case, the work is done by a real human professional, not by AI.

Are you the cheapest option?

We don’t claim that. We offer low starting rates, and in development, design, and writing those rates are often well under typical USD-posted marketplace figures we checked in late 2026. Admin support is priced from $10/hr; in that sample the posted midpoint was USD 10/hr, so we don’t use the “well under” line for admin. The quote for your brief is the price that matters.

How does the $50 cash voucher work?

Every new member gets a $50 cash voucher, used against their first job. One voucher per new customer. We’ll show it on the quote. It is not redeemable for cash, and you can’t transfer it.

How do I pay?

After you accept a written quote, we send a card payment link for that amount. We don’t take payment before the scope is agreed. If our payment account is still being set up, we’ll tell you in the reply rather than invent another method.

What currency are the rates in?

All prices are in Australian dollars, including starting rates, quotes, and the $50 cash voucher. We are not GST-registered, so GST is not added.

What won’t you take?

In-person local tasks (the kind often posted on local task apps). Writing, completing, or submitting schoolwork, exams, or hiring take-home tests. Videos or files you don’t have the right to use. Promises of sales, search rank, or app-store placement.

Do you have reviews on this site?

Not yet. We won’t invent reviews, ratings, or client stories.
